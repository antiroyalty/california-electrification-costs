CLAIM 2

Full electrification with properly-sized solar beats staying on gas. It wins in 46 of 47 California counties

Statewide median savings: 14.6% annualized cost, relative to staying on gas with solar. The one exception is Alpine County, because it has the highest heat-pump installation cost of any county in the dataset.

Tests backing this claim

  • TestHeatPumpSpaceCost and TestHeatPumpWaterHeaterCost (4 tests, capital_costs_test.py). Every one of the 47 pipeline counties’ heat pump costs is in a plausible range, and a missing county falls back to a computed median rather than a silent guess.
  • TestCARBAppliancesCost (4 tests, capital_costs_test.py). Induction stove and gas appliance costs match the underlying CARB data.
  • TestGasVsElectricCostOrdering (3 tests, capital_costs_test.py). Electric appliances cost more upfront than their gas equivalent in every county, the direction a reviewer would sanity-check first.
  • nem3_billing_test.py (24 tests). The NEM 3.0 billing math itself: export credit rates, fixed charges, carry-forward, cross-checked against the utility’s own published bill examples for 10 rate-plan and utility combinations.
  • energy_consumption_ballpark_test.py (30 tests). The underlying energy-use assumptions are benchmarked against EIA RECS, CEC RASS, and NREL ResStock survey data, not invented load shapes.
  • test_composite_key_preserves_other_scenarios_for_same_county (merge_and_write_csv_test.py). The exact bug class that caused an earlier data-loss incident, one county’s row silently overwriting another’s, can’t happen in the ledger these numbers come from.

Sources

  • Heat pump space heating & water heating. TECH Clean California program data (CEC), per-county contractor/turnkey cost, ducted systems only for space heating.
  • Induction stove, gas appliances. CARB appliance comparison data, 2022 costs inflated 3.4% CPI-U to 2023.
  • EV / ICE vehicle costs. DOE incremental purchase-cost methodology (2022); ICCT maintenance & insurance data.
  • Solar and battery sources as in Claim 1.
On the Alpine County exception: Alpine has the highest heat-pump space-heating cost of any county in the underlying TECH Clean California data ($24,962 vs. a statewide median of ~$19,400). The co-optimizer builds a smaller system than the fixed-PV approach would, but even that smaller, cost-minimizing system still costs slightly more than staying on gas there.
