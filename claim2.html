CLAIM 2

Full electrification with co-optimized PV/storage has lower modeled annual cost than the gas-appliance + ICE reference in 46 of 47 counties

This is a like-for-like comparison of the repository’s baseline_ice_car reference and full_electric_ev_coopt case. The reference retains gas space/water heating and cooking plus an internal-combustion vehicle; it also retains the fixed PV/storage convention used by the non-co-optimized scenario family, so it is not a no-solar household.

Calculation

EAC = annualized PV + annualized storage + annualized electric equipment + annualized gas equipment + electricity bill + gas bill + vehicle O&M. Claim 2 reports 100 × (reference EAC − co-optimized electric EAC) / reference EAC.

Source: analysis_results/claims_eac_by_county_nem3_g1146e05.csv. The builder requires exactly one finite, non-negative row for each of the three cases in every one of the repository’s 47 counties; missing or duplicate coverage fails the build.
