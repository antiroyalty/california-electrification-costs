Tests backing this claim

  • co_optimization_test.py (3 tests). The co-optimizer’s reported capacities and cost components reconcile with each other, so a mis-wired reporting layer can’t quietly show one number while computing another.
  • solar_storage_dispatch_test.py (6 tests). The hour-by-hour dispatch physics (load balance, battery state-of-charge bounds, no double-counted energy) hold at every timestep, for every county.
  • step23_compare_coopt_vs_fixed_dispatch_test.py. The specific fixed-vs-co-optimized comparison this claim rests on reads the right cost fields.
  • TestLPAnalyticalBaseline (lp_cooptimize_test.py). The LP’s no-solar baseline case matches a closed-form calculation by hand, the simplest possible check that the optimizer isn’t just fitting noise.
